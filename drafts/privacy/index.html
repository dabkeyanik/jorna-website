Jorna is a marketplace for planning South Asian celebrations. Clients assemble a team of vendors — venue, catering, DJ, dhol, mehndi, photography — and pay through escrow, so money is held until the event happens. This page explains what we collect to make that work, who else sees it, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Account details — name, email, username, and optionally a phone number, age, city and profile photo To create your account, show you to the people you book with, and contact you about bookings
Vendor profile — business description, category and speciality, service listings, prices, photos, travel radius, Instagram handle So clients can find and book you. This is public within the app
Event and booking details — dates, times, guest counts, venue address and its map location, budget To match vendors, calculate prices, and run the booking
Precise location — your device's GPS position, only at the moment you tap to check in at an event To confirm someone is actually at the venue before payment is released. It is not collected in the background and not tracked between check-ins
Content you create — group chat messages, reviews, photos you upload To run the conversation and the reviews other people rely on
Payment records — amounts, currency, status, timestamps, and the identifiers Stripe gives us To run escrow, refunds, disputes and vendor payouts
Device tokens — a push notification token per device To tell you a booking was approved, an offer arrived, or a message came in
Google Calendar access — for vendors who choose to connect it To read your busy times so double-bookings don't happen. You can disconnect at any time

We never see your card number

Card details are entered on Stripe's own hosted checkout page and are never sent to, or stored on, Jorna's servers.

How the AI bundle builder uses your event

When you ask Jorna to build vendor bundles, the details you entered — city, date, guest count, budget and the style you picked — are sent to a third-party language model provider (currently OpenRouter) to help assemble the options. We do not send your name, email, phone number, messages or payment details.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

  • Other Jorna users — the people you book with see what they need to: your name, profile photo, the booking's details, and anything you write in a shared chat or a review.
  • Stripe — payments, escrow and vendor payouts.
  • Supabase — sign-in (including Google sign-in) and photo storage.
  • Google — if you sign in with Google, or connect Google Calendar.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications.
  • OpenRouter — the AI bundle builder, as described above.
  • Cloudflare and Railway — hosting the site and the API.
  • Legal — if we're required by law, or need to investigate fraud, abuse or a safety issue.

How long we keep it

Account and booking records are kept while your account exists, and afterwards only where we must — completed transactions, for example, have to be retained for tax and accounting purposes, and Stripe keeps its own records under its own policy.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. In the app: Profile → Delete Account. This removes your account and personal profile. Where a record must survive — a completed payment, for instance — it is retained only as required.
  • Correct your details. Profile → Account Settings.
  • Location. Decline the permission and check-in simply won't be available; nothing else changes.
  • Notifications. Turn them off in your device settings at any time.
  • Google Calendar. Disconnect from your vendor calendar screen.
  • Block or report someone. Available on any profile or message. Blocked users disappear from your search results, home feed, messages and reviews.
  • Access or a copy of your data. Email us and we'll help.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights — to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing. Contact us and we will honour them.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored hashed and salted, never in readable form. Sign-in tokens are held in the device keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever affects your data we will tell you.
